Crochet Frog Dice Bag

Designed by Heather Heisler

Watch the video tutorialPrint this pattern

This playful frog-shaped dice bag combines amigurumi character design with practical storage. The main pouch is worked first and finished with a drawstring closure hidden inside the frog’s wide-open mouth. Dimensional stuffed eyes, front arms, and sculpted hind legs are added afterward, transforming a functional pouch into a whimsical tabletop gaming companion.

Materials

  • Yarn: medium weight (category 4). 1 skein green (or preferred frog color); small amount of white (optional, for eyes)
  • Hook: US size H/8 (5.00 mm)
  • Notions: safety eyes, yarn needle, 25 in paracord, 1 toggle/barrel fastener, polyester fiberfill stuffing

Abbreviations

  • ch chain
  • dc double crochet
  • hdc half double crochet
  • hdc2tog half double crochet 2 stitches together (decrease)
  • rep repeat
  • rnd(s) round(s)
  • sc single crochet
  • sc2tog single crochet 2 stitches together (decrease)
  • sl st slip stitch
  • st(s) stitch(es)

Phase 1: Bag body

Using green yarn:

Rnd 1Ch 2, 10 hdc in 2nd ch from hook; join with sl st to first hdc. (10 hdc)
Rnd 2Ch 1, [2 hdc in first st, hdc in next st] rep around; join with sl st to first hdc. (15 hdc)
Rnd 3Ch 1, [2 hdc in first st, hdc in next st] rep around; join with sl st to first hdc. (23 hdc)
Rnd 4Ch 1, [2 hdc in first st, hdc in next st] rep around; join with sl st to first hdc. (35 hdc)
Rnds 5–13Ch 1, hdc in each st around; join with sl st to first hdc. (35 hdc)
Rnd 14Ch 1, [hdc2tog, hdc in next 2 sts] rep around, ending the final repeat with 1 hdc; join with sl st to first hdc. (26 hdc)
Rnd 15Drawstring channel: Ch 2, 1 dc in each st around; join with sl st to first dc. (26 dc)

Fasten off and weave in all ends.

Drawstring assembly

  • Take your 25 in piece of paracord.
  • Thread the cording in and out through the spaces of the Rnd 15 dc row.
  • Slide both ends of the cord through your toggle fastener.
  • Tie a secure knot at the end of the paracord to prevent the toggle from slipping off.

Phase 2: Features and limbs

Eyes (make 2)

Using your choice of green or white yarn:

Rnd 1Ch 2, 6 sc in 2nd ch from hook; join with sl st to first sc. (6 sc)
Rnd 2Ch 1, [2 sc in first st, sc in next st] rep around; join with sl st to first sc. (9 sc)
Rnd 3Ch 1, [2 sc in first st, sc in next st] rep around, ending with 2 sc in the last st; join with sl st to first sc. (14 sc)
Rnds 4–6Ch 1, sc in each st around; join with sl st to first sc. (14 sc)
Rnd 7Ch 1, [sc2tog, sc in next st] rep around, ending with sc2tog; join with sl st to first sc. (9 sc)

Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing. Insert your safety eyes into the pieces. Feel free to place them wherever you like to achieve your preferred expression (for example cross-eyed or wide-set). Stuff the eye firmly with fiberfill and sew it securely to the top of the body.

Front arms (make 2)

Using green yarn:

Rnd 1Ch 2, 4 sc in 2nd ch from hook; join with sl st to first sc. (4 sc)
Rnds 2–6Ch 1, sc in each st around; join with sl st to first sc. (4 sc)

Fasten off, leaving a long tail, and sew the arms to the sides of the body.

Back legs (make 2)

Using green yarn:

Rnd 1Ch 2, 8 sc in 2nd ch from hook; join with sl st to first sc. (8 sc)
Rnd 2Ch 1, 2 sc in each st around; join with sl st to first sc. (16 sc)
Rnd 3Ch 1, [2 sc in first st, sc in next st] rep around; join with sl st to first sc. (24 sc)
Rnds 4–7Ch 1, sc in each st around; join with sl st to first sc. (24 sc)
Rnd 8Ch 1, [sc2tog, sc in next st] rep around; join with sl st to first sc. (16 sc)
Pause here to begin stuffing the leg with fiberfill.
Rnd 9Ch 1, sc2tog around; join with sl st to first sc. (8 sc)
Rnds 10–12Ch 1, sc in each st around; join with sl st to first sc. (8 sc)

Finish adding any necessary stuffing. Flatten the opening and sc the seam shut to close. Fasten off, leaving a long tail, and sew the legs securely to the lower base of the body.
